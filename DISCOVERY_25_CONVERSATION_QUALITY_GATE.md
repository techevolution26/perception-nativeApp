# Discovery 25 — Conversation Quality Gate

Mobile acceptance gate carried forward from the conversation-first product architecture.

- Topic carousel remains the primary Home discovery surface.
- Topic names retain dedicated space and are not blocked by the For You / Recommendations control.
- For You and Recommendations remain compact navigation rather than competing feeds.
- Related Perceptions are user-initiated after meaningful engagement and sustained attention.
- Moderation, reporting, privacy, verification, and abuse controls remain server-authoritative.
- This stage adds no new Home information stream.
